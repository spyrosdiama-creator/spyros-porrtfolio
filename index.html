Ferrero Rocher
Ice Cream

This business consultancy project responded to a live brief from Ferrero, tasked with reversing the decline of its Premium Sticks range by rebuilding value perception, awareness, and distribution, all within HFSS advertising restrictions. My contribution focused on the PESTEL analysis and secondary research that shaped our recommended strategic route, alongside designing the OOH and social visuals used to pitch the concept to stakeholders.
